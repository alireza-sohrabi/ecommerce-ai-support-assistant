# Order Tracking Guide

Use this guide to understand LumaTrail Goods order statuses, locate a shipment, and determine when to contact support.

## Where to find tracking

When a shipment leaves the warehouse, LumaTrail Goods emails a shipping confirmation containing the carrier name and tracking link. Signed-in customers can also open **Account → Orders**, select the order, and view each shipment.

An order can have more than one tracking number when products ship from different locations.

## Status definitions

- **Order received:** The order was accepted but warehouse processing has not started.
- **Processing:** Inventory is being confirmed and prepared for packing.
- **Partially shipped:** At least one package has left the warehouse, while another item is still processing.
- **Shipped:** The carrier has accepted the package.
- **Delivered:** The carrier recorded delivery at the destination.
- **Cancelled:** The order or remaining unshipped items will not be dispatched.

## Tracking has not updated

A new label may take up to 24 hours to receive its first carrier scan. Temporary gaps can also occur between distribution centers. Contact support when there has been no tracking update for 5 business days.

## Changing or cancelling an order

Contact support within 30 minutes of checkout to request a cancellation or address correction. Requests cannot be guaranteed once processing begins. Shipped orders cannot be cancelled and must follow the returns policy after delivery.

## Getting help

Provide support with:

- the order number
- the email address used at checkout
- the delivery postal code
- the affected tracking number, if available

Do not send account passwords, one-time login codes, or complete payment-card details.

If tracking says delivered but the package is missing, follow the delivered-package steps in the shipping policy before opening an investigation.
