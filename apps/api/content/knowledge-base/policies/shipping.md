# Shipping Policy

LumaTrail Goods ships apparel, footwear, and everyday accessories from fulfillment locations in the United States. This policy applies to orders placed through the LumaTrail Goods online store.

## Order processing

- In-stock orders are prepared within 1–2 business days.
- Orders placed after 2:00 p.m. Central Time begin processing the next business day.
- Weekends and public holidays are not processing days.
- Items may ship separately when they are stored in different locations. Each shipment receives its own tracking link.

## Delivery options

| Service | Estimated transit time | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 3–7 business days | Available for residential addresses and PO boxes |
| Express | 1–2 business days | Not available for PO boxes or some remote areas |

Transit estimates begin after dispatch and are not guaranteed delivery dates. The available services and exact charges appear at checkout. Standard shipping is free when the merchandise subtotal is at least $75 after discounts and before taxes.

## Address changes

Customers should contact support within 30 minutes of placing an order to request an address correction. A change cannot be guaranteed after warehouse processing begins. Support cannot redirect a package after the carrier accepts it.

## Delayed, lost, or missing packages

- Allow up to 24 hours for a new tracking number to show its first carrier scan.
- Contact support if tracking has not updated for 5 business days.
- If tracking says delivered, check household members, nearby entrances, and the carrier's delivery notes, then wait 24 hours before reporting the package missing.
- Missing-package reports must be submitted within 14 days of the recorded delivery date.

Support may open a carrier investigation before offering a replacement or refund. Customers should provide the order number and delivery postal code, but should never send passwords or complete payment-card details.

## Shipping regions

LumaTrail Goods currently ships only within the continental United States. International shipping and freight-forwarding addresses are not supported.
