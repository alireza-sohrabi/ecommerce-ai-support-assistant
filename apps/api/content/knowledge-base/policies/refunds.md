# Refund Policy

LumaTrail Goods issues refunds after an eligible cancellation or an approved return. Refund timing depends on warehouse inspection and the customer's financial institution.

## Return inspection

Returns are normally inspected within 3–5 business days after the warehouse records delivery. The inspection confirms the returned item and its condition against the approved return request.

If a return does not meet the return policy, support contacts the customer before deciding whether the item can be sent back. Return-shipping charges may apply.

## Refund method and timing

- Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method.
- Customers receive an email when the refund is submitted.
- Banks and card issuers commonly take another 5–10 business days to post the credit.
- Original shipping charges are not refundable unless the store shipped the wrong item or the product arrived damaged or defective.
- A $6.95 return-label fee is deducted when applicable under the returns policy.

Orders paid with both a gift card and another payment method are refunded to the gift card first, up to the amount originally charged to it.

## Partial refunds

A partial refund may apply when an eligible product is returned with missing accessories, damaged retail packaging, or another condition that reduces its resale value. Support will explain the adjustment before the refund is finalized.

## Cancellations and authorization holds

When an order is cancelled before payment capture, the pending authorization may disappear rather than appear as a separate refund. Most financial institutions release authorization holds within 3–7 business days. The customer's bank controls the final timing.

## Missing refunds

If 10 business days have passed since the refund-confirmation email:

1. Check the original payment account and recent transaction history.
2. Contact the bank or card issuer with the refund date and amount.
3. Contact support if the bank cannot locate the credit.

Support can confirm the refund reference but will never ask for a password, security code, or complete payment-card number.
