# Returns Policy

LumaTrail Goods accepts eligible returns that meet the time and condition requirements below. This policy applies to purchases made through the LumaTrail Goods online store.

## Return window

Eligible products may be returned within 30 calendar days of the recorded delivery date. A return must be requested before the window closes.

## Item condition

Returned products must be:

- unused, unworn, and unwashed
- free from odors, stains, pet hair, and customer-caused damage
- returned with original tags, accessories, and packaging
- packed so the product and retail packaging are protected in transit

Footwear must include the undamaged original shoe box. The shoe box must not be used as the shipping container.

## Items that cannot be returned

The following items are final sale:

- gift cards
- personalized or made-to-order products
- products marked “Final Sale” before purchase
- opened hygiene-sealed goods
- items damaged through misuse or ordinary wear

This policy does not limit rights that cannot be excluded under applicable consumer law.

## Starting a return

Contact support with the order number and the items being returned. After eligibility is confirmed, support provides a return authorization and shipping instructions. Unapproved packages may be delayed or returned to the sender.

A $6.95 return-label fee is deducted from the refund. The fee is waived when the store sent the wrong product or the item arrived damaged or defective.

## Exchanges

Direct exchanges are not offered because inventory can change while a return is in transit. Customers may return an eligible item and place a separate order for the replacement size or color.

## Return shipping

Packages should be handed to the specified carrier before the authorization expires. Customers should keep the carrier receipt and tracking number until the return is completed. The store is not responsible for returns sent with an unauthorized label or without tracking.
