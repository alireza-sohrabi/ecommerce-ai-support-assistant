# Product FAQs

These answers cover common questions about LumaTrail Goods apparel, footwear, and everyday accessories.

## How do I choose a size?

Use the size chart on the individual product page because measurements can differ between product families. Compare the chart with measurements from a similar item that fits well. If a measurement falls between sizes, check the product description for slim, regular, or relaxed fit guidance.

## Where can I find materials and care instructions?

The product page lists the primary materials and care guidance. The sewn-in or printed care label on the delivered item is the final authority. Following a different washing, drying, ironing, or cleaning method may damage the product.

## Will product colors look exactly like the photos?

LumaTrail Goods photographs products under controlled lighting, but screens and lighting conditions can change how colors appear. Small color variations are normal. A clearly incorrect color should be reported to support with the order number and product name.

## What does “low stock” mean?

“Low stock” means only a small quantity is currently available; it does not reserve an item. Inventory is reserved only after checkout succeeds. An item can sell out while it is in a shopping cart.

## Can I reserve an out-of-stock item?

Reservations and backorders are not available. When offered, the product page's restock notification can send an email after inventory returns. A notification does not guarantee availability or hold the item.

## What should I do if an item arrives damaged or incomplete?

Contact support within 7 days of delivery. Include the order number, product name, a description of the problem, and clear photos of the item and shipping package when practical. Keep the product and packaging until support provides instructions.

## Are replacement parts available?

Replacement accessories or parts are available only for selected products. Contact support with the product name, order number, and the part needed. Support will confirm availability and whether a charge applies.

## Are product reviews verified?

A “Verified Purchase” label means the review account has a completed order containing that product. Reviews without the label may still come from customers who purchased through another channel or received the product as a gift.
